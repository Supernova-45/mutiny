# mutiny design

The normal/mutant structural comparison leads; explanations live in the evidence drawer. Visible text must identify biology, evidence, measurements, or a meaningful action. Do not narrate camera behavior or interface mechanics. Titles ask a concrete cancer-biology question; avoid slogans, italic word emphasis, numbered navigation, oversized metrics, and rounded card layouts.

Warm ivory (#f3f1ec) is the canvas, charcoal (#252727) the text, copper the response/mutation accent. Use DM Sans for navigation and labels, IBM Plex Mono for sequences and measurements. Preserve distinct unresolved-pool and missing-data marks.

The secondary vaccine-study view uses one patient per row on an open canvas, with the target inspector separated by a single rule. The heading is “Which cancer mutations trigger T cells?” The entry page asks “What changes beyond a cancer mutation?” and uses two large synchronized structures, a short prediction/reveal choice, and a mutation → W6 → receptor path. Local comparisons keep the same paired view and add a residue-wise difference figure. Personalization means visitor predictions, notes and saved views, without inventing patient-specific outcomes. Mobile stacks the structures. Camera movement must never suggest molecular dynamics or an interpolated experimental state. Automatic rotation is opt-in; expanded mode exits with Escape.

Source guidance: [Impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable), particularly distill and craft-floor. Scientific constraints remain in docs/SCIENCE.md.
