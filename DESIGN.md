# Callback design

The atlas and experimental structures lead; explanations live in the evidence drawer. Keep visible copy to controls, measurements, identities and essential scientific boundaries.

Warm ivory (#f3f1ec) is the canvas, charcoal (#252727) the text, copper the response/mutation accent. Use DM Sans for navigation and labels, IBM Plex Mono for sequences and measurements. Preserve distinct unresolved-pool and missing-data marks.

The atlas uses one patient per row with a selected-target inspector. Recognition uses two large synchronized experimental structures, a compact toolbar, and a residue measurement strip. Mobile stacks the structures. Camera movement must never suggest molecular dynamics or an interpolated experimental state. Automatic rotation is opt-in; expanded mode exits with Escape.

Source guidance: [Impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable), particularly distill and craft-floor. Scientific constraints remain in docs/SCIENCE.md.
